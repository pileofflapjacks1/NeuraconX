    Before you begin

    NeuraconX is a browser-based research and accessibility prototype for browsing a placeholder Neurabeach catalog with intention-friendly controls. It is an X-related app in Joe’s Neura suite.

    • It is not a medical device and is not software as a medical device (SaMD).
    • It does not connect to Neuralink or any implant hardware.
    • It is not affiliated with Neuralink or any implant vendor.
    • Keyboard and mouse act as the intention simulator in this version.
    • Downloads and launches are simulated locally for feedback only.
